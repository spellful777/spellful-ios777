## App Store Connect fields that require owner confirmation

- **Seller / copyright holder:** Enter the legal owner shown in your Apple Developer account. Do not invent a legal entity.
- **Support URL:** The published privacy page contains contact information, but a dedicated working support page is preferable. Verify App Store Connect accepts the selected page.
- **Age rating:** Complete Apple's actual content questionnaire, accounting for community/user-generated content if accessible.
- **Privacy labels:** Audit the distributed app and any embedded website, SDKs, analytics, or tracking; do not automatically select "Data Not Collected."
- **Pricing and purchases:** Confirm whether the app is free and whether any purchasable features are implemented.
- **Screenshots:** Capture authentic screens from the finished app in Apple's supported screenshot sizes; design mockups are not substitutes.
- **Export compliance, signing, bundle ID, testing:** Complete during the final Codemagic/App Store Connect setup.
