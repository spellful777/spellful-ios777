## Which policy applies?

Spellful has an existing published Privacy Notice at **https://spellful.com/privacy**. This is the official public policy URL to enter in App Store Connect, provided the website operator confirms it also accurately covers the shipping iOS app and its associated services.

This document describes the **proposed native iOS starter** and the relationship to the website. It is not a replacement for the full published legal notice. Before releasing the app, the owner must verify and, if necessary, update the website policy so it clearly covers the iOS app's actual data practices.

## Information handled by the native iOS starter

The supplied prototype was designed to save a display name, username, zodiac-related profile fields, biography, and private grimoire/journal entries on the user's own device. The starter design does not include a separate developer-hosted account backend or a native analytics SDK. Those details must be confirmed by inspecting the final compiled app and its included dependencies.

A locally stored journal entry is not automatically uploaded to Spellful's website by this starter. Removing the app generally removes local app data, subject to device backups and platform behavior.

## Visiting the Spellful website

Opening the community link takes the user to spellful.com. Use of Spellful's website, accounts, social/community tools, and any paid features is covered by the website's published Privacy Notice and Terms of Use. Depending on the user's activity, the website privacy notice explains that Spellful may process account and contact information, user-generated posts, device and usage information, cookies, payment-related information when purchases occur, and information from third parties.

The website privacy notice also discusses service providers and potential analytics/advertising partners. This does **not** establish that every listed category is collected by the native starter app itself. App Store privacy disclosures must be based on what the actual submitted app and its integrations do.

## User choices and contact

Users can edit the locally saved profile and journal fields provided by the prototype. Questions about the website, its data practices, privacy choices, or technical support can be directed to **legal@spellful.com**, as stated in the published website notice. The full website notice explains additional options, including marketing-email choices and cookie controls.

## Children's privacy and security

The website Privacy Notice says the website is not directed toward children under 13 and provides information about security and international data processing. Read the full live notice for the site's detailed terms. Choose the iOS app's age rating only after completing Apple's questionnaire for the real release.

## Required before App Store release

1. Verify that https://spellful.com/privacy is live and accurately covers **both** the website and the finished iOS application.
2. Verify whether the app adds login, synchronization, crash reporting, analytics, push notifications, advertising, payment providers, or other third-party SDKs.
3. Complete App Store Connect privacy disclosures for the exact production build.
4. If the app supports account creation, verify the necessary account deletion path and community safety/reporting/blocking measures.
5. Confirm the publishing legal entity, support details, and any necessary regional privacy rights notices with the website operator.
